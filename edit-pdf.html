Edit a PDF — cover a word or add text

If a PDF has a mistake — a wrong name, an old date, a word that should not be there — FileNora lets you cover it with a white box, or add new text in its place. You do not need to install anything, and you do not need the original document.

Drag a box over the word to cover it, or click a spot on the page, type your text, and place it there. You can move through the pages with Prev and Next, and your edits on each page are kept while you work.

The new text will look close to the original, but an exact font match is not always possible — PDFs often embed their original font, which the browser cannot reuse. FileNora detects the approximate size of the text on the page and uses the closest standard font. Everything runs in your browser; your file is never uploaded.

How to do it

  1. Open the tool below and pick "Remove a word from a PDF".
  2. Add your PDF, then tap "Open the editor".
  3. Drag over the word to cover it — or click a spot, type new text, and tap "Place text".
  4. Tap "Save edited PDF", wait 10 seconds, then download your file.

Questions

Will the new text match the original font exactly?

Not always. PDFs often embed their original font, which the browser cannot reuse. FileNora detects the approximate size and colour of the text on the page and uses the closest standard font — the match is best-effort.

Can I edit more than one page?

Yes. Use Prev and Next to move between pages. Edits are stored per page, so moving away and back never loses what you did.

Can I save with several pages on one sheet?

Yes. Next to "Save edited PDF" you can pick 1, 2 or 4 pages on each sheet. With 2 or 4, several pages are shrunk onto each sheet of the saved PDF.
