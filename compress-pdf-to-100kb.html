Compress a PDF to 100 KB

Many forms ask for a file under 100 KB, and a scanned PDF or a phone photo is often much bigger. FileNora shrinks your PDF or photo to the size you pick — 50 KB, 100 KB, 150 KB, all the way to 1 MB, or a size you type yourself. 100 KB is already picked for you, because that is what most forms ask for.

Everything happens in your browser, on your own device. Your file is never uploaded anywhere, there is no account to make, and there is no watermark on the result. If your file is already under the size you picked, we tell you so and give you back your original — we never make a file bigger.

If the size you picked cannot be reached without the pages becoming unreadable, we say so plainly and show the real final size, so you can try a slightly bigger target instead.

How to do it

  1. Open the tool below and pick "Make a file smaller".
  2. Add your PDF or photo.
  3. Pick a size — 100 KB is already selected — then tap "Make it smaller".
  4. Wait 10 seconds for the download to unlock, then download your file.

Questions

Will my file be exactly 100 KB?

Usually it comes out just under your target, but not always exactly. Text-heavy PDFs shrink well; scanned photos may not reach a very small size and stay readable. FileNora always shows the real final size.

What if my file is already small enough?

Then nothing needs shrinking. FileNora tells you so and gives you back your original file, unchanged.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every tool on FileNora is free. There is a 10-second wait before a download unlocks — that short wait is how the site stays free.
