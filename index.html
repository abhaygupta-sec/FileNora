    How to do common jobs

    Compress PDF to 100 KB

    Add your PDF, choose “Compress to an exact size”, pick 100 KB, and click Run. FileNora re-renders the pages at lower quality until the file fits just under 100 KB. If the file cannot reach 100 KB without becoming unreadable, FileNora shows the smallest size it could reach and says so.

    Merge PDF

    Add two or more PDFs, choose “Merge files into one PDF” in the “What do you want to do?” list, then click “Run selected job”. When the merge prompt appears, click “Merge into one PDF”, drag the files into the order you want — first, second, third — then click “Merge in this order”. You get one PDF, in that order.

    JPG to PDF

    Add JPG, PNG or WebP photos, choose “JPG / PNG / WebP to PDF”, and click Run. Each photo becomes a page in one PDF, in the order you added them.

    PDF to JPG

    Add a PDF, choose “PDF to JPG / PNG”, pick JPG or PNG in the “Image format” box, and click “Run selected job”. Each page is saved as an image you can download.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is FileNora really free?

    Yes. All tools on this page are free. There is a 10-second wait before a download unlocks — that is how the site stays free.

    Do my files get uploaded anywhere?

    No. Every tool runs in your browser, on your device. Your files never leave your device, and nothing is stored on a server.

    Do I need an account?

    No. There is no sign-in, no account, and no email required.

    Can I get an exact file size, like exactly 100 KB?

    Usually we can get just under your target, but not always exactly. Text-heavy PDFs compress well; scanned photos may not reach a very small target. We always show the real final size and tell you honestly if the target was not reached.

    Why can’t the edited text match the original font exactly?

    PDFs often embed their original font, which the browser cannot reuse. FileNora detects the approximate size and colour of the text on the page and uses the closest standard font. The match is best-effort.

    What file types can I use?

    In this version: PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, DOCX and TXT.
