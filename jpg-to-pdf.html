Turn JPG photos into a PDF

Photos of documents from your phone often need to go into a form as one PDF. FileNora turns your photos into a single PDF — each photo becomes a page, in the order you added them. JPG, PNG and WebP all work.

You can pick the page size. "Fit to photo" makes each page the size of the photo. "A4" puts each photo in the middle of an A4 page, which suits forms that expect A4 pages.

Everything runs in your browser, on your own device. Your photos are never uploaded, there is no account to make, and the PDF has no watermark.

How to do it

  1. Open the tool below and pick "Turn photos into a PDF".
  2. Add your photos — JPG, PNG or WebP.
  3. Pick a page size: "Fit to photo" or "A4".
  4. Tap "Turn photos into a PDF", wait 10 seconds, then download your file.

Questions

What is the difference between "Fit to photo" and "A4"?

"Fit to photo" makes each page the size of the photo itself. "A4" puts each photo, centred, on an A4 page. Both keep the photo's proportions.

What order do the photos go in?

The order you added them. The first photo you add becomes the first page of the PDF.

Is there a limit on how many photos I can add?

There is no fixed count. Each file can be up to 50 MB, and more than one file at a time is fine.
