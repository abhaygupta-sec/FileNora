Merge PDF files into one PDF

If you need your marksheet and certificate in one file, or several pages scanned as separate files, FileNora joins them into a single PDF. Add two or more files, put them in the order you want with the Up and Down buttons, and tap one button.

Photos work too — a JPG, PNG or WebP becomes a page in the merged PDF, so you can mix scans and photos in one file. You can also choose to put 2 or 4 pages on each sheet, which shrinks several pages onto one sheet of the merged PDF.

Everything runs in your browser, on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and the merged PDF has no watermark.

How to do it

  1. Open the tool below and pick "Join files into one PDF".
  2. Add 2 or more files — PDFs, photos, or a mix of both.
  3. Put them in order with the Up and Down buttons.
  4. Tap "Join into one PDF", wait 10 seconds, then download your file.

Questions

Can I change the order of the files?

Yes. The first file becomes the first pages of the merged PDF. Use the Up and Down buttons next to each file to change the order before you tap join.

Can I merge photos as well as PDFs?

Yes. JPG, PNG and WebP photos are added as pages in the merged PDF, in the order you added them.

What does "pages on each sheet" do?

With 1, each page stays on its own sheet, as normal. With 2 or 4, several pages are shrunk onto each sheet of the merged PDF. It only applies when you join 2 or more files.
