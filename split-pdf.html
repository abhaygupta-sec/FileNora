Split a PDF — keep only the pages you need

If a PDF has pages you do not need — a blank page, an extra form, or pages that belong to someone else — FileNora makes a new PDF with only the pages you keep. Your original file is never changed.

Tick the pages you want to keep, or type a page range like "1-3, 5" to keep pages 1, 2, 3 and 5. The kept pages stay in their original order.

Everything runs in your browser, on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and the new PDF has no watermark.

How to do it

  1. Open the tool below and pick "Keep only some pages".
  2. Add your PDF.
  3. Tick the pages to keep, or type a range like "1-3, 5" and tap Apply.
  4. Tap "Keep these pages", wait 10 seconds, then download your new PDF.

Questions

How do I write a page range?

Use numbers and dashes, separated by commas — for example "1-3, 5" keeps pages 1, 2, 3 and 5. If a page does not exist, FileNora tells you.

Does splitting change my original PDF?

No. Splitting makes a new PDF with the pages you kept. Your original file stays as it was.

Can I keep just one page?

Yes. Tick a single page and that page alone becomes the new PDF.
